🎯 Goals Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

Goals Tracker is a personal goal-tracking app. This page explains what data it handles and how.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — goal titles, steps, completion status, check-ins, and streaks — is saved locally in your browser or device's storage. By default this data never leaves your device.

If you tap the sync button (🔗) to enable cross-device sync, that same data is also copied to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), under a random 8-character code you choose to share between your own devices. This is optional and off unless you turn it on.

What is not collected

There is no account system, login, or sign-up. We never collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal identifier. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties. Firebase/Google Cloud acts only as the storage provider for the optional sync feature, governed by Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data — Goals: Habit & Goal Tracker

Everything Goals: Habit & Goal Tracker stores lives in one of two places, and either can be deleted:

  • On-device data (goal titles, steps, streaks — stored locally in the app): clear the app's storage/data from your Android device settings, or uninstall the app. This deletes it immediately and permanently; nothing is kept anywhere else unless sync was ever turned on.
  • Synced data (only exists if you tapped the 🔗 sync button): email ankitylearn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my synced data" and include the 8-character sync code shown in the app's sync screen. The corresponding record is deleted from our Firebase database, usually within a few days. No other copy of it is retained anywhere.

We don't retain any data beyond what's described above — there's no account system and no backups kept after a deletion request.
